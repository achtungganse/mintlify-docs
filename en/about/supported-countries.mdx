eBanano covers 100+ destinations — from weekend city breaks to round-the-world adventures. Whether you're sipping espresso in Rome, island-hopping in Southeast Asia, or road-tripping across the Americas, chances are we've got you covered. Here's an overview of where you can connect.

## Coverage by Region

- 🌍 **Europe** — Wide coverage across EU countries, the UK, Switzerland, Norway, Turkey, and more. Regional plans make multi-country European trips a breeze.
- 🌏 **Asia-Pacific** — Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Australia, New Zealand, and many more. Perfect for long-haul adventures across the region.
- 🌎 **Americas** — USA, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, and more. Whether it's a business trip to New York or a backpacking journey through South America, we've got a plan for you.
- 🌍 **Middle East & Africa** — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, South Africa, Kenya, and more. Coverage is growing fast across these regions, so check back often.

## How to Check If Your Destination Is Covered

Not sure if we serve your next stop? It takes about ten seconds to find out:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to ebanano.com">
    Open the eBanano website or app on your phone or computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Destination">
    Type your destination country or region into the search bar on the homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Available Plans">
    Any plans available for that country will appear right away, complete with pricing and data allowances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Not Listed?">
    If no plans appear, that destination isn't supported just yet. But keep reading — that might change sooner than you think!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Our coverage is always expanding. If your destination isn't listed today, check back before your next trip — new countries are added regularly. You can also drop us a message if there's somewhere specific you'd love to see us add.
</Info>

## What Does a "Regional Plan" Cover?

<Accordion title="What is a regional plan and which countries does it include?">
  A regional plan covers multiple countries under a single data allowance — perfect if you're hopping between destinations on one trip. For example, a Europe regional plan might cover 30+ countries, so you don't need to buy a separate plan each time you cross a border.

  Regional plans are a popular choice for:

  - **European road trips** — drive through France, Spain, Italy, and beyond on one plan
  - **Southeast Asia adventures** — Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, and more in a single package
  - **Multi-city business travel** — stay connected across several countries without managing multiple eSIMs

  When you search for a destination, both local (single-country) and regional plans will appear where available, so you can compare and choose what suits your itinerary best.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  For multi-country trips, always check both local and regional plan options. A regional plan often works out better value than buying individual country plans — especially if you're visiting three or more countries in one go.
</Tip>
