Creating an eBanano account is completely free and takes less than a minute. Once you're set up, you'll have one place to manage all your eSIM plans, access your QR codes from anywhere, earn Bananos rewards on every purchase, and breeze through checkout on your next adventure. Think of it as your personal travel HQ. 🌍

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit ebanano.com and tap Sign Up">
    Head to [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) and click or tap the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and create a password">
    Type in your email address and choose a strong password. Make it something memorable — you'll use these details every time you log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from eBanano. Click the link inside to confirm your address. If you don't see it within a minute or two, check your spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in!">
    That's it — your account is ready to go. You can now browse plans for your next destination and make your first purchase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Prefer a quicker option?** You can also sign up instantly using your **Google** or **Apple** account — no password needed. Just tap the Google or Apple button on the sign-up page and you'll be ready in seconds.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You don't need an account to buy a plan — but having one lets you access your order history, view and resend eSIM QR codes, earn and redeem Bananos, and enjoy faster checkouts every time you travel.
</Note>

## What You Get with an Account

Once you're signed up, a whole lot of handy perks open up:

- 📱 **View and resend eSIM QR codes** — misplaced the email? No problem, it's always in your account.
- 📊 **Track data usage** across all your active and past plans.
- 🍌 **Earn and redeem Bananos** — eBanano's loyalty rewards on every purchase.
- ⚡ **Faster checkout** for future trips — your details are saved and ready to go.
