Android and eBanano are a great travel pairing — as long as your phone supports eSIM, you're good to go. The exact steps to install vary a little depending on who made your phone, but the overall journey is the same: find your SIM settings, scan your QR code, and you're done. This guide covers **Samsung Galaxy** and **Google Pixel** in detail, with tips for other Android brands too.

## Install Your eSIM

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Samsung Galaxy">
    Most Samsung Galaxy phones released since 2019 support eSIM, including the S20 series and later, as well as select A-series and Z Fold/Flip models.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings and go to Connections">
        Open the **Settings** app and tap **Connections**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap SIM Manager">
        Tap **SIM Manager** to view and manage the SIMs on your device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Add eSIM or Add Mobile Plan">
        Tap **Add eSIM** (on newer Samsung UI) or **Add mobile plan** (on some older versions). The exact label may vary slightly by model or Android version.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Scan QR Code">
        Select **Scan QR code** and point your camera at the QR code in your eBanano confirmation email.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow on-screen prompts to confirm">
        Your phone will download the eSIM profile. Tap through the confirmation prompts — Samsung may ask you to give the eSIM a name or confirm it as your active data SIM.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enable Data Roaming">
        Once installed, go to **Settings > Connections > Mobile Networks** and turn on **Data Roaming**. Make sure eBanano is selected as your active data SIM. Without roaming enabled, you won't get data abroad.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Pixel">
    Google Pixel phones have supported eSIM since the Pixel 2, and the setup process is one of the most straightforward on Android.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings and go to Network & Internet">
        Open the **Settings** app and tap **Network & Internet**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap SIMs">
        Tap **SIMs** to manage your current SIM cards and add new ones.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the Plus Button or Add SIM">
        Tap the **\+** icon or the **Add SIM** option at the bottom of the SIMs screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Download a SIM Instead">
        Your Pixel will ask if you want to insert a physical SIM or download one. Tap **Download a SIM instead?** to proceed with eSIM installation.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Scan your QR code">
        Point your camera at the QR code from your eBanano email. The Pixel's scanner is quick — it should recognise the code within a second or two.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow the prompts and enable Data Roaming">
        Tap through the confirmation steps. Once complete, go to your mobile network settings and turn on **Data Roaming** for your eBanano eSIM so it can connect to local networks when you travel.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Using a different Android brand?** If you have a **OnePlus**, **Motorola**, **Sony**, or another Android phone, the steps will be similar — just the menu names might differ. Look for **eSIM**, **Mobile Plan**, or **Add SIM** inside your network or SIM settings. If you're stuck, your phone manufacturer's support page or our [eBanano Help Centre](https://ebanano.com/help) will have model-specific guidance.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Before you head to the airport, go into your SIM settings and confirm that eBanano is set as your **mobile data SIM**. It only takes a second and saves you from landing abroad and wondering why nothing is loading.
</Tip>
