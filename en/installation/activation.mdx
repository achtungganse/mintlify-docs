Here's something that trips up a lot of first-time eSIM travellers: **installation and activation are two completely separate steps.** Installing your eSIM is something you do at home, before your trip. Activation — when your data plan actually switches on and starts counting — happens when you arrive at your destination. Think of it like loading your travel card at home, then tapping it when you get on the train.

## What Is Activation?

When you install your eBanano eSIM, you're simply downloading the plan onto your phone. Your data allowance hasn't started yet, and you haven't used a single megabyte. Activation is the moment your plan "wakes up" and begins. This is great news: it means you can install days or even weeks before your trip without worrying about your data running out before you've even boarded the plane. **Your plan's validity period starts on the day of first activation — not on the date you purchased it.**

## How Activation Works

Depending on your specific eBanano plan, activation is either fully automatic or requires a quick manual nudge.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Automatic Activation">
    Most eBanano plans are set to activate automatically — which means there's genuinely nothing you need to do. 🎉

    As soon as your phone lands in your destination country and connects to a local mobile network, eBanano detects the connection and activates your plan in the background. Within a minute or two of landing, you should see data bars appear on your eBanano eSIM line and your plan will be live.

    **Tips for smooth automatic activation:**

    - Make sure your eBanano eSIM is enabled (not toggled off) before you land
    - Confirm that Data Roaming is switched on for your eBanano line (see the [iOS](./ios) or [Android](./android) guides)
    - If you're on Airplane Mode during your flight, turn it off once you've landed and give your phone 60–90 seconds to connect to a local network
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual Activation">
    Some eBanano plans — particularly longer-duration or multi-country plans — require you to manually enable the eSIM when you're ready to start using it. Your confirmation email will let you know if this applies to your plan.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="iPhone: Go to Settings > Cellular">
        Open **Settings**, tap **Cellular** (or **Mobile Service**), and find your eBanano eSIM in the list of plans.
      </Step>
      <Step title="iPhone: Turn on this line">
        Tap your eBanano eSIM and toggle **Turn On This Line** to the on position. Your plan will activate and your data will become available shortly after.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Android (Samsung): Go to Settings > Connections > SIM Manager">
        Open **Settings**, go to **Connections**, tap **SIM Manager**, and find your eBanano eSIM.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Android: Enable your eBanano eSIM">
        Tap the toggle next to your eBanano eSIM to enable it. On Google Pixel, you'll find the same option under **Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs**. Once enabled, your plan activates and data will flow within a minute or two.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  **Your plan validity starts on the day of first activation, not on your purchase date.** So whether you buy your eSIM two weeks in advance or the night before your flight, your data days don't start counting until you actually switch on and use the plan abroad. No pressure, no rush — install whenever is convenient.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting Activation

If things aren't going quite to plan, don't panic. Here are the most common hiccups and how to sort them out.

<Accordion title="My eSIM is installed but not activating">
  First, run through this quick checklist:

  - **Is your eBanano eSIM enabled?** Check your Cellular (iOS) or SIM Manager (Android) settings and make sure the line is toggled on.
  - **Is Data Roaming turned on?** This is the most common culprit. Head to your eSIM's settings and enable Data Roaming — it's required for connecting to local networks abroad.
  - **Is your phone connected to a network?** Swipe down to check signal bars. If you see "No Service", try toggling Airplane Mode on and off to force your phone to search for available networks.
  - **Is eBanano set as your data SIM?** Make sure your phone is routing data through the eBanano eSIM line, not your home SIM.

  If you've checked all of the above and still have no data, please reach out to [eBanano Support](https://ebanano.com/help) — we're always happy to help you troubleshoot in real time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I accidentally activated too early — can I get an extension?">
  We know it happens! If you believe your plan activated before you intended — for example, if your phone connected to a roaming network unexpectedly — contact our [support team](https://ebanano.com/help) as soon as possible and explain what happened. While we can't guarantee extensions in every case, we review these situations individually and do our best to make things right. Getting in touch quickly gives us the best chance to help.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I know my eSIM is active?">
  There are a few easy ways to check:

  - **Signal indicator:** When your eBanano eSIM is active and connected, you'll see signal bars and a data indicator (4G, LTE, or 5G) next to the eBanano line on your status bar or in your SIM settings.
  - **Try loading a page:** Open your browser and visit any website. If it loads, you're live!
  - **Check your eBanano account:** Log in at [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) to view your plan status, activation date, and remaining data balance in real time.
</Accordion>
