The eBanano referral program is one of the best ways to earn Bananos — share your unique link and get rewarded every time a friend buys their first plan 🍌. There's no limit to the fun: the more travel-loving friends you have, the more Bananos stack up in your account. And your friends get a great deal too, so really, everyone wins.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your eBanano account">
    Visit [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) and sign in. You'll need an account to access your unique referral link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to 'Referrals' in your profile menu">
    Tap your profile icon in the top corner and select **Referrals** from the menu. This is your referral dashboard — it shows your unique link, how many friends you've referred, and your earned Bananos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your unique referral link">
    Tap **Copy Link** to grab your personal referral URL. This link is unique to your account, so every signup through it is tracked back to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with friends">
    Send your link wherever your friends hang out — WhatsApp, Telegram, email, Instagram, Facebook, travel forums — anywhere! The more people who see it, the more chances to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn Bananos when your friend makes their first purchase">
    When your friend clicks your link, signs up, and completes their first eBanano purchase, you'll both receive Bananos as a reward. It's that simple.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Does Each Person Get?

When a referral is successful, both you and your friend benefit:

- **You (the referrer):** Earn a set number of Bananos once your friend completes their first purchase. The exact amount is shown in your referral dashboard.
- **Your friend (the new user):** They may receive a welcome discount or bonus Bananos on their first purchase — the current offer is always displayed on the referral landing page they reach through your link.

Check your **Referrals** dashboard for the up-to-date reward amounts — these can change with promotions and seasonal offers.

<Tip>
  The best places to share your referral link? Travel WhatsApp groups, expat communities, digital nomad forums, and your social media. People who are already thinking about travel are the most likely to sign up — and they'll thank you for the tip!
</Tip>

<Info>
  Referral Bananos are credited to your account **after your friend's first purchase is confirmed** — not when they simply sign up. So encourage your friends to go all the way and buy their first plan to unlock the reward for both of you!
</Info>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="How many friends can I refer?">
  There's no cap on the number of friends you can refer — the more the merrier! Every friend who signs up and makes their first purchase through your link counts as a successful referral and earns you Bananos.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My friend signed up but I didn't get my Bananos">
  Referral Bananos are only credited once your friend completes their **first purchase** — signing up alone doesn't trigger the reward. If your friend has made a purchase and you still haven't received your Bananos after a few hours, please check the following:

  - Confirm your friend used your referral link when signing up (not a different link or direct sign-up).
  - Make sure it was their first eBanano purchase.

  If everything checks out and you still haven't received your Bananos, contact our support team with your referral details and we'll investigate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I refer someone who already has an eBanano account?">
  Unfortunately, no. The referral reward is only triggered when a brand new user signs up through your link and makes their first purchase. If someone already has an existing eBanano account, they won't count as a new referral.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a referral limit per month?">
  There is no monthly cap on referrals — you can refer as many new friends as you like, any time. However, eBanano reserves the right to review unusual referral activity. Referrals must be genuine new users; any attempt to game the system may result in Bananos being removed and account review.
</Accordion>
