The more you travel with eBanano, the more Bananos you collect — and there are several ways to make that pile grow faster. Whether you're buying your own plans, spreading the word to friends, or snagging a seasonal bonus, every Banano is one step closer to a free (or cheaper) trip. Here's how each earning method works.

## Ways to Earn Bananos

- 🛒 **Purchases** — Earn Bananos automatically on every eBanano plan you buy. The more you spend, the more you earn — it's that straightforward. No codes, no opt-ins required.
- 👥 **Referrals** — Invite a friend to eBanano and earn Bananos when they complete their first purchase. Your friend gets a great deal, and you get rewarded for the introduction. Win-win. 🍌
- 🎉 **Promotions** — Keep an eye out for seasonal sales, special events, and limited-time campaigns that award bonus Bananos. Check the app or subscribe to notifications so you never miss one.

## How Many Bananos Do I Earn Per Purchase?

You earn Bananos based on how much you spend — for every set amount spent on an eBanano plan (for example, per dollar or equivalent), you'll receive a set number of Bananos in return. The exact earning rate is always displayed clearly at checkout and in your account's rewards section, so you always know exactly what you're getting before you buy.

<Tip>
  **Stack your earnings!** Make a purchase using your referral link with a friend and combine it with a promotional period — that way you rack up purchase Bananos _and_ referral Bananos at the same time. More travel, more Bananos.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Bananos are credited to your account after a purchase is successfully completed. If an order is refunded for any reason, the Bananos earned from that purchase will be reversed from your balance accordingly.
</Info>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="Did I earn Bananos on a guest checkout?">
  Unfortunately, Bananos can only be earned when you're logged in to an eBanano account at the time of purchase. Guest checkouts are not eligible for Bananos rewards.

  If you created an account after making a guest purchase, those Bananos can't be applied retroactively — but all future purchases made while logged in will earn rewards. It's a good reason to create a free account before you buy!
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When are Bananos added to my account?">
  Bananos are added to your balance automatically once your purchase is confirmed — usually within a few minutes of completing checkout. You'll be able to see the new balance in your account under **My Bananos**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I didn't receive Bananos for my purchase — what do I do?">
  First, make sure you were logged in to your account at the time of purchase (guest orders don't earn Bananos). If you were logged in and still haven't seen your Bananos credited after a few hours, please reach out to our support team with your order number and we'll look into it right away.
</Accordion>
