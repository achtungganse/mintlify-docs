Bananos are eBanano's loyalty currency — a fun way to be rewarded for every trip you take and every friend you bring along 🍌. Every time you buy a plan, refer a pal, or catch a special promotion, you'll collect Bananos that you can put toward your next adventure. Think of them as your frequent flyer miles, but way more tropical.

## How Bananos Work

It's simple: earn Bananos, spend Bananos. Here's the full picture:

- 🛒 **Earned on purchases** — Every plan you buy earns you Bananos automatically. No hoops to jump through.
- 👥 **Earned through referrals** — Invite friends to eBanano and earn Bananos when they make their first purchase.
- 🎉 **Earned in promotions** — Keep an eye out for seasonal events and special campaigns that award bonus Bananos.
- 🏷️ **Redeemed at checkout** — Use your Bananos balance as a discount when you're buying your next plan. Apply all of them or save some for later — it's up to you.

## What Is a Banano Worth?

Each Banano is worth a small fraction of a cent toward your next eBanano purchase. The exact exchange rate — meaning how much discount you get per Banano — is always shown clearly at checkout and in your account's rewards section, so you'll never be left guessing.

<Note>
  Bananos are not a currency and cannot be transferred or withdrawn as cash. They can only be used to get discounts on eBanano purchases. They're a thank-you from us — not a bank account!
</Note>

## Do Bananos Expire?

<Accordion title="When do Bananos expire?">
  Bananos are designed to reward active travelers. If your eBanano account has **no activity for 12 consecutive months** (no purchases, no logins, no redemptions), any Bananos in your balance will expire.

  To keep your Bananos alive, simply stay active — even logging in or browsing plans counts. If you're planning a big trip, your balance will be right there waiting for you.

  If you think your Bananos expired by mistake, get in touch with our support team and we'll take a look.
</Accordion>

## Where to See Your Bananos Balance

Curious how many Bananos you've stacked up? Here's how to check:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your eBanano account">
    Visit [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your profile">
    Tap your profile icon or name in the top corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find 'My Bananos' or the Rewards section">
    Look for the **My Bananos** tab or the **Rewards** section in your account menu. Your current balance and recent Bananos activity will be displayed there.
  </Step>
</Steps>
