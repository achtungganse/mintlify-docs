eBanano uses eSIM technology to give you local data wherever you travel — without swapping SIM cards or paying roaming charges. Think of it as having a local phone plan in your pocket before you even leave home. No queues, no plastic chips, no nasty bill surprises when you get back.

## What Is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM card built right into your phone. Instead of a tiny plastic chip you slot in and out, your eSIM lives in your phone's software. That means you can store multiple carrier profiles on one device — your home plan and a travel plan, side by side — and switch between them in seconds. Most modern smartphones already have one built in, waiting to be used.

## Your eBanano Journey

Getting connected abroad is a five-step adventure (a very quick one, we promise):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Plans">
    Head to the eBanano website or app and search by destination. You'll see all the available data plans for your country or region, with pricing up front — no hidden fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    Pick your plan and pay securely. Your eSIM is delivered straight to your email inbox within seconds. Yes, seconds — you'll have time to make a cup of tea before you need to do anything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Open the email on your phone and scan the QR code to save the eSIM to your device. Do this before your trip while you're still on Wi-Fi at home — it only takes a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Your plan activates automatically when you land in your destination country. Prefer to be in control? You can also switch it on manually in your phone's settings whenever you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy">
    You're connected! Fast, local data in 100+ countries — and your home SIM still handles your calls and texts as usual. Best of both worlds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why eBanano? Let's Compare

Not all travel data options are created equal. Here's how eBanano stacks up against the alternatives:

- ❌ **Roaming** — Your home carrier charges a premium for roaming — often eye-wateringly expensive. Setup is instant but your wallet will feel it. Not ideal for longer trips.
- ⏳ **Local SIM** — A local SIM can offer great value, but it means hunting for a phone shop at the airport, queuing, and fiddling with a paperclip to swap cards. Not exactly the arrival experience you dreamed of.
- ✅ **eBanano** — Instant delivery, affordable pricing, and zero SIM swapping. Buy it at home, install it in minutes, and arrive already connected. Travel sorted.

<Note>
  eBanano plans are **data-only** — they cover mobile internet on your device. Your home SIM stays active in the background and continues to handle your calls and SMS messages as normal.
</Note>
