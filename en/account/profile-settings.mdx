Your profile settings are your personal control panel — it's where you keep your account info up to date, change your email or password, and set your preferred language and currency. Whether you've moved, changed your email provider, or just want to switch to a different language, it's all just a few taps away.

## How to Access Profile Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your eBanano account">
    Visit [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) and sign in with your email and password (or your Google/Apple account if you signed up that way).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap your profile icon or name">
    Look for your profile icon or your name in the top corner of the screen. Give it a tap or click to open your account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Account Settings or Profile">
    Choose **Account Settings** or **Profile** from the dropdown menu to open your settings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Change

Once you're in your profile settings, here's what you can update:

- **Display name** — Change how your name appears in the app and in emails.
- **Email address** — Update to a new email address any time.
- **Password** — Set a new password whenever you like. We recommend updating it periodically for security.
- **Preferred language** — Choose between **English**, **Russian**, and **Kazakh** to use eBanano in the language that suits you best.
- **Currency preference** — Set your preferred display currency so prices always show up the way you expect.

<Note>
  After changing your email, you'll receive a verification link at your new address. Make sure to click it to confirm the change — until you do, your old email remains active.
</Note>

## Deleting Your Account

<Accordion title="How do I delete my eBanano account?">
  We're sorry to see you go! If you'd like to permanently delete your account, please reach out to our support team — account deletion can't be done directly from the app, so just drop us a message and we'll take care of it for you.

  **Before you request deletion, please keep in mind:**

  - Your entire order history will be permanently removed.
  - Any unused Bananos in your balance will be forfeited and cannot be recovered.
  - Active eSIM plans will no longer be accessible through your account.

  If you're just having a problem with the app or a plan, our support team might be able to help sort things out without needing to delete your account!
</Accordion>
