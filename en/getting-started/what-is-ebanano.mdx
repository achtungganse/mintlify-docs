eBanano is your pocket-sized travel companion for staying connected wherever your adventures take you. It's a travel eSIM service that lets you buy a data plan online, receive it instantly by email, and get online in 100+ countries — all without touching a physical SIM card. No hunting for a local SIM at the airport, no jaw-dropping roaming bills when you get home. Just tap, scan, and go.

## What Is an eSIM?

An eSIM (short for _embedded SIM_) is a digital SIM card built directly into your phone. Instead of sliding a tiny plastic chip into your device, you download a plan wirelessly and activate it in seconds. Your phone can store multiple eSIM profiles at once, so you can keep your home number active while using eBanano for data abroad — the best of both worlds.

<Note>
  eSIMs are a data-only technology on eBanano. You'll stay connected to the internet, but calls and SMS will still use your regular home plan (or a VoIP app like WhatsApp or FaceTime).
</Note>

## How eBanano Works

Getting connected with eBanano is refreshingly simple:

1. **Browse plans** — search by destination to find local, regional, or global options.
2. **Purchase** — checkout takes under 5 minutes, and payment is secure.
3. **Receive your eSIM** — a QR code lands in your inbox instantly.
4. **Install & activate** — scan the QR code in your phone's settings and you're ready to roam.
