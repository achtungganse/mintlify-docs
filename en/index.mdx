## How eBanano Works

Getting online abroad has never been easier. Here's the whole journey in four steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Browse data plans by country or region on [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com). Pick the size and duration that fits your trip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Purchase">
    Pay securely with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or your Bananos balance. You'll receive your eSIM details instantly by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Your eSIM">
    Scan the QR code (or tap the installation link) before your trip. Your eSIM is saved to your phone — no physical card needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrive and Connect">
    Switch on your eSIM in your phone's settings when you land. That's it — enjoy fast local data in 100+ countries. 🌍
  </Step>
</Steps>
