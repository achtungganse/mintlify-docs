Not all trips are the same, and neither are eBanano's plans. Whether you're spending two weeks on a single island, hopscotching across Europe, or embarking on a round-the-world adventure, there's a plan built for your journey. eBanano offers three plan types — local, regional, and global — and choosing the right one comes down to three simple questions: where are you going, how long are you staying, and how much data do you actually need?

## How to Decide: A Quick Decision Guide

Still not sure which plan is right for you? Answer these three questions:

**1. How many countries are you visiting?**

- Just one → go with a **Local Plan**
- A few in the same region → go with a **Regional Plan**
- Multiple regions or lots of countries → go with a **Global Plan**

**2. How long is your trip?**

- Shorter trips (a few days to a week) benefit most from a focused Local or Regional plan where you can get exactly the data you need.
- Longer or open-ended trips may suit a Global plan for flexibility, or a series of top-ups on a Regional plan.

**3. How much data do you typically use?**

- Check the section below for help estimating your weekly data needs — it's easier than you think!

## How Much Data Do I Actually Need?

This is the question everyone asks. The honest answer: it depends on how you use your phone. Here are some helpful benchmarks:

<Accordion title="Light use — 1 to 2 GB per week">
  You check Google Maps to find your next destination, send messages on WhatsApp, look up opening hours, and maybe scroll through your emails. You're not streaming video or uploading large files.

  **1–2 GB per week** is plenty for this kind of trip. A local plan with 1–3 GB is a smart, affordable choice.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Moderate use — 3 to 5 GB per week">
  You're posting Stories to Instagram, jumping on a few video calls with family or colleagues, browsing the web regularly, and using navigation throughout the day.

  **3–5 GB per week** covers this comfortably. A regional plan in the 5–10 GB range is a safe bet for a 1–2 week trip.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Heavy use — 10+ GB per week">
  You're streaming music or podcasts on the go, watching videos, working remotely with video meetings, or uploading lots of content. You basically want your phone to behave as if you're at home on Wi-Fi.

  **10 GB or more per week** is a realistic estimate. Look at larger data packages or a global plan with generous allowances — and keep an eye on top-up options just in case.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Don't stress about picking the perfect amount of data upfront. If you run out, you can **top up your eBanano plan** at any time — no need to buy the biggest package just to feel safe. Start with what seems reasonable and add more if you need it.
</Tip>
