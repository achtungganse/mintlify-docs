Knowing how much data you have left is one of those small things that can make a big difference on the road. Whether you're navigating a new city, sharing photos from a mountaintop, or just keeping in touch with home, staying on top of your balance means no nasty surprises — and no frantic searching for Wi-Fi when you need it most.

## Check Your Data Balance

You can check your remaining data in two ways: straight from the eBanano app, or through your phone's built-in settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the eBanano App">
    The app is the easiest way to see exactly how much data you have left — and when your plan expires.

    1. Open the **eBanano app** on your phone.
    2. Tap **My eSIMs** from the home screen.
    3. Tap your **active plan** to open the plan details.
    4. You'll see your **remaining data** and your plan's **expiry date** right at the top.

    The app updates your balance in real time, so what you see is what you've got.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On Your Phone">
    Your phone also tracks data usage natively. The numbers here reflect all data used on that SIM line, so it's a handy cross-check.

    **iPhone:**

    1. Go to **Settings** → **Cellular**.
    2. Scroll down and tap your **eBanano eSIM** line.
    3. View **Current Period** usage stats at the bottom of the screen.

    > Note: iPhone usage stats reset manually — tap **Reset Statistics** at the bottom to start fresh when your plan begins.

    **Android:**

    1. Go to **Settings** → **Connections** → **Data Usage**.
    2. Tap your **eBanano SIM** from the list of lines.
    3. View your data usage over the current period.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Heading somewhere remote — a national park, a mountain trail, a small island? Check your balance before you go. It's much easier to top up while you still have a signal!
</Tip>

## Data Usage Tips

Wondering where all your data went? These quick tips will help you stretch every megabyte a little further.

<Accordion title="How to reduce data usage while travelling">
  A few simple habits can go a long way:

  - **Download offline maps** (Google Maps, Maps.me) before you leave your hotel's Wi-Fi.
  - **Turn off background app refresh** — apps love to quietly update themselves in the background. On iPhone: Settings → General → Background App Refresh. On Android: Settings → Apps → your app → Battery → Background usage.
  - **Stream at lower quality** — if you're watching videos on the go, drop the resolution to 480p. You'll barely notice the difference; your data balance will.
  - **Use Wi-Fi whenever it's available** — cafés, hotels, and airports are your friends.
  - **Disable auto-play videos** in social media apps like Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does WhatsApp or iMessage use data?">
  Yes — both WhatsApp and iMessage send messages and calls over the internet, so they do use your mobile data when you're not on Wi-Fi.

  The good news: text messages use a tiny amount of data (just a few kilobytes each). Voice and video calls use more — roughly **1–3 MB per minute** depending on quality.

  To keep usage low, switch to voice notes or text instead of video calls when you're on the go.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does my plan include calls or SMS?">
  eBanano plans are **data-only**. They don't include traditional phone calls or SMS text messages.

  For calls and texts, you have two great options:

  - Keep your **home SIM active** in your phone alongside your eBanano eSIM — your regular number stays reachable for calls and texts.
  - Use **internet-based apps** like WhatsApp, FaceTime, Telegram, or Signal for messaging and calls over data.

  Either way, you're covered!
</Accordion>
