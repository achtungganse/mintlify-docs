Once you've built up a Bananos balance, it's time to put them to work. Redeeming Bananos is simple — just apply them at checkout and watch your total drop. You can use your whole balance in one go or save some for a bigger purchase down the line. Your Bananos, your call.

## How to Redeem Bananos at Checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a plan to your cart">
    Browse plans on [ebanano.com](https://ebanano.com) or in the app, find the perfect plan for your destination, and add it to your cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceed to checkout">
    When you're ready to buy, tap **Checkout** to move to the payment page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    You must be logged in to redeem Bananos. If you're not already signed in, you'll be prompted to log in or create an account at this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the 'Use Bananos' toggle or checkbox">
    On the checkout page, find the **Use Bananos** option — it'll show your current Bananos balance alongside the discount it represents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many Bananos to apply">
    Toggle on the full amount, or adjust the slider or input field to apply only a portion of your balance. The discount will update in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your purchase">
    Once you're happy with the discount applied, finish checkout as normal. Your Bananos are deducted and your savings are locked in. Enjoy the trip!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to spend all your Bananos at once. If you're saving up for a longer or pricier trip, apply just a few now and let the rest keep growing. Every little bit counts!
</Tip>

## Can I Use Bananos and a Promo Code Together?

<Note>
  Yes! Bananos and promo codes are fully compatible — you can apply both at checkout to stack your savings. Add your promo code first, then toggle on your Bananos for maximum discount. Double the savings, double the fun. 🍌
</Note>

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="How much is my Bananos balance worth?">
  The value of your Bananos balance is shown clearly at checkout in your local currency equivalent. You can also check your balance and its current value at any time by going to **My Bananos** in your account profile. The exact conversion rate may vary slightly with promotions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My Bananos didn't apply at checkout">
  A few things to check:

  - Make sure you were **logged in** to your account before reaching checkout — Bananos can't be applied on guest orders.
  - Check that you actively toggled on the **Use Bananos** option (it isn't applied automatically by default).
  - Ensure your Bananos balance hasn't expired due to account inactivity.

  Still having trouble? Reach out to our support team with your order details and we'll sort it out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I transfer Bananos to someone else?">
  Bananos are tied to your individual eBanano account and cannot be transferred to another account. They're personal rewards — a thank-you just for you. That said, you can always share your referral link so friends can start earning their own!
</Accordion>
